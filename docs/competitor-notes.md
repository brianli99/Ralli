### What stands out by product

#### TeamSnap
- Onboarding: Team-first creation with roles (coach/parent/player) makes intent clear from first screen.
- Event creation: Templates for practices/games; recurring schedules; availability requested by default.
- RSVP/check-ins: One-tap yes/no/maybe; automatic availability reminders; clear headcount.
- Chat & notifications: Team chat threads and announcement-type notifications; per-event reminders.
- Discovery: Little to none; product is inward-facing to existing teams.
- Trust/safety: Roster privacy and coach/admin controls; parental controls for youth teams.
- Rankings/achievements: Light stats and attendance; not a core loop.
- Shareability: Invite links for teams and events; calendar sync.

#### Heja
- Onboarding: Quick team join via code; parent/guardian flows are simple.
- Event creation: Practice/game presets with auto-reminders; repeat scheduling.
- RSVP/check-ins: One-tap availability; late/absence reasons; visual headcount.
- Chat & notifications: Team chat and per-event comment threads; granular notification settings.
- Discovery: Minimal; focused on existing teams.
- Trust/safety: Roster visibility controls; guardian approval for youth.
- Rankings/achievements: Light stats only.
- Shareability: Team invite codes; calendar export.

#### OpenSports
- Onboarding: Clear split for players vs organizers; simple payment setup for hosts.
- Event creation: Strong for pickup sessions, leagues, tournaments; repeat events and caps.
- RSVP/check-ins: Spots remaining, waitlists, auto-refunds; check-in and penalty policies.
- Chat & notifications: Event chat and group chat; automated reminders and payment notices.
- Discovery: Map/list of nearby sessions with filters (sport, time, price, level).
- Trust/safety: Host/org ratings, refund policies, ID for payouts.
- Rankings/achievements: Not core; some league standings.
- Shareability: Public event links, promo codes; easy share to messaging apps.

#### Meetup
- Onboarding: Join groups based on interests and location; quick account creation.
- Event creation: Simple event composer with caps, waitlist, and fees.
- RSVP/check-ins: RSVP with capacity and waitlist; reminders and iCal.
- Chat & notifications: Limited; comments per event and group announcements.
- Discovery: Strong local discovery with categories and time-based filters.
- Trust/safety: Group longevity, member counts, and reviews serve as social proof.
- Rankings/achievements: None.
- Shareability: Strong social sharing and invite flows.

#### SportsEngine
- Onboarding: Admin/league setup flows; teams and divisions first.
- Event creation: Robust scheduling (games/practices/tournaments) with conflict detection.
- RSVP/check-ins: Roster attendance tracking; coach views and exports.
- Chat & notifications: Team messaging, announcements, and schedule change alerts.
- Discovery: Minimal public discovery; org-centric.
- Trust/safety: Background checks, compliance tools for youth sports.
- Rankings/achievements: Standings, stats, and scores are first-class.
- Shareability: Team/league sites and schedule embeds.

#### CourtReserve / Playtomic (racquet)
- Onboarding: Club-first and player accounts; NTRP/level capture improves matchmaking.
- Event creation: Courts, clinics, ladders; time slots and recurring programming.
- RSVP/check-ins: Booking with live availability; check-in/late cancel policies; waitlists.
- Chat & notifications: Limited messaging; strong transactional notifications (booking/reminders).
- Discovery: Nearby clubs/courts with pricing and availability; filters by surface/amenities.
- Trust/safety: Verified clubs, payments held, penalties for no-shows.
- Rankings/achievements: Ladders, ratings, match history.
- Shareability: Shareable booking/event links.

#### Nike Run Club (social/run)
- Onboarding: Fast account creation; quick-start run; optional device pairing.
- Event creation: Focus on solo runs/challenges; limited group event tooling.
- RSVP/check-ins: N/A for events; run streaks and scheduled reminders for workouts.
- Chat & notifications: Cheers and coaching prompts; achievement notifications.
- Discovery: Challenges and programs; light local discovery through partners.
- Trust/safety: Not a core pillar; standard reporting.
- Rankings/achievements: Badges, streaks, personal records.
- Shareability: Strong social sharing of runs and achievements.

#### Strava (social fitness)
- Onboarding: Social graph and interests upfront; quick device/app connections.
- Event creation: Clubs and events exist but are secondary; most activity is organic.
- RSVP/check-ins: Limited; focus on recording and uploads rather than RSVPs.
- Chat & notifications: Comments, kudos, club announcements; rich notification settings.
- Discovery: Excellent via segments, routes, clubs; map-first exploration.
- Trust/safety: Verified athletes, privacy zones, report tools.
- Rankings/achievements: Deep leaderboards, segments, trophies.
- Shareability: Extensive social sharing and embeds; auto-share to platforms.

### Must-have UX cues to mirror (6–10)
- Frictionless RSVP: one-tap availability with clear state (going/maybe/not) and undo.
- Clear capacity indicators: live spots left, waitlist status, and over-cap warnings.
- Proactive reminders: smart timing (T-24h/T-2h) plus optional day-of check-in nudges.
- Clean empty states: concise copy, example cards, and a primary "Create your first session" CTA.
- Inline payments clarity: price, fees, refund/cancel policy, and penalties shown before commit.
- Credibility signals: host/venue badges, ratings, repeat-organizer labels, and friends-attended.
- Map-first discovery: nearby sessions with filters (time, level, distance, price) and quick-book.
- Smart defaults: remember last venue/time/skill level; prefill based on past behavior.
- Social proof & share: show friends going, counts, recent activity; one-tap share links.
